## Diopter AI

**Real-time defense against AI social engineering and deepfakes.** [diopter.ai](https://diopter.ai)

Attackers use AI to become someone your team trusts. Diopter catches it on live calls, before money or access changes hands. It checks identity, spots social engineering tactics, detects AI-generated voice and video, and flags policy breaches while the call is still live.
